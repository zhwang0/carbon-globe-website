01 THE DATASETGROUNDED IN ECOLOGY. PREPARED FOR AI.

The next frontier
is on the ground.

Digital twins of the Earth need to represent how forests grow, store carbon, and respond to environmental change. Learning to emulate these dynamics brings the living land into the picture.

CarbonGlobe is the first global-scale, ML-ready dataset for forest carbon forecasting, bringing decades of simulations, harmonized inputs, and reproducible benchmarks to the ML community.

04 TOWARD A DIGITAL EARTHLEARN THE DYNAMICS. BUILD THE NEXT MODEL.

The forest layer
of a digital Earth.

A digital twin of the Earth needs models of its living systems as well as its atmosphere. AI ecosystem emulators offer a way to learn those dynamics from process-based simulations.

CarbonGlobe provides the foundation today: reference sequences for training, eight baseline models, and benchmarks for testing how well an emulator follows decades of forest change.

01 / TRAIN

Learn an ecosystem.

Develop emulators from meteorological drivers, CO₂, soil conditions, and a starting forest state. Explore architectures that incorporate ecological knowledge.

Start with the baselines
02 / BENCHMARK

Make decades count.

Evaluate against ED reference outputs. Measure state errors, annual changes, and the final forecast state as small errors accumulate over long rollouts.

Explore the benchmark
03 / CONNECT

Build toward a fuller Earth.

Investigate how ecosystem emulators could connect with weather and climate models, bringing forest carbon responses into future digital twins.
