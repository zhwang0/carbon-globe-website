The included source file is unchanged. export_globe_surface.py rasterizes land
polygons at the center of each 0.5-degree cell. Natural Earth supplies independent
land/ocean geometry; it supplies no forest carbon values. Carbon values originate
from CarbonGlobe ED simulations, with limited display interpolation labeled in
globe-surface.json metadata and its provenance mask.
